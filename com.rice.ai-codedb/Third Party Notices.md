# Third Party Notices

UnityCodeDB does not bundle third-party provider source or binaries.

The package can invoke `killop/codedb-mcp` / `codebase-mcp` as an external
process dependency supplied by the host project. That provider remains governed
by its upstream license and distribution terms. Node.js and Windows PowerShell
are also external runtime prerequisites and are not redistributed by this
package.
